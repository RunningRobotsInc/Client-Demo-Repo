## Q3 Capacity Risk
As noted in the April 28 Q1 Review, Operations Manager Dave Holm flagged a strategic concern: at current RFQ growth rates (+41.3% YoY), Prairie Ridge will approach full shop capacity in Q3 2026. The 5-axis cell — at 94% and already the primary scheduling constraint — has the least room to absorb additional demand.

**Options under consideration:**
1. Second shift on 5-axis cell (requires certified machinist hire or cross-training)
2. Subcontracting overflow for simpler 3-axis work to free internal capacity for 5-axis
3. Selective quoting — prioritize higher-margin 5-axis and complex weld work, decline commodity laser jobs
