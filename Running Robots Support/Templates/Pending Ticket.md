# Pending Ticket Template

Use this when an issue has come in and Alex is actively working on it.
Open with acknowledgment of the problem, reassure the client, and set a clear expectation.

---

## Framework

[Acknowledge what the client reported — one sentence confirming you've seen it and it's being looked into.]

[Reassure them — let them know the team is on it and they don't need to do anything.]

[Set expectation — give a timeframe if you have one, or let them know you'll follow up once it's resolved.]

[Sign-off]

Larissa Simbro
Running Robots Support
support@therunningrobots.com

---

## Example

Thanks for reaching out — we've seen your message and are already looking into the issue with your site.

Our team is on it and working to get things sorted out for you. You don't need to do anything on your end.

We'll follow up as soon as we have it resolved. [Optional: We're aiming to have this taken care of by [timeframe].]

Talk soon,

Larissa Simbro
Running Robots Support
support@therunningrobots.com

---

## Notes
- Keep the tone calm and reassuring — the client should feel like they're in good hands
- If you have a realistic timeframe, include it — it reduces follow-up emails from the client
- If you don't have a timeframe, don't guess — just say you'll be in touch once it's resolved
- Never go into technical detail about what's being worked on unless the client specifically asks
