# Escalation Notice Template

Use this when an issue is beyond the scope of what you can resolve, or requires Tif's involvement.
The client should feel reassured and confident that the right people are on it — not alarmed.

---

## Framework

[Acknowledge what the client reported — confirm you've looked into it.]

[Reassure them — let them know this is being taken seriously and escalated to make sure it gets the attention it deserves.]

[Set expectation — let them know Tif is looping in and they'll be in touch. Avoid making specific promises on timing unless you're confident in them.]

[Sign-off]

Larissa Simbro
Running Robots Support
support@therunningrobots.com

---

## Example

Thanks for flagging this — I've looked into it and want to make sure we handle it the right way for you.

I'm bringing Tif in on this one — this is exactly the type of issue she specializes in, and I want to make sure you're getting the right person on it. This is being treated as a priority.

Tif will be reaching out to you directly [soon / by (timeframe) / to schedule time to talk through it]. In the meantime, please don't hesitate to reply here if anything changes or you have additional questions.

Talk soon,

Larissa Simbro
Running Robots Support
support@therunningrobots.com

---

## Notes
- Tone should be serious but calm — the client should feel reassured, not alarmed
- Don't over-explain what the issue is or speculate on causes — keep it focused on next steps
- Avoid language that implies fault or blame (Running Robots' or the client's)
- If Tif has already been looped in, say so — it signals the team is already moving
- Only commit to a timeframe if you've confirmed it with Tif first
