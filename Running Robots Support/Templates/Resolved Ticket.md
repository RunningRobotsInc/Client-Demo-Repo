# Resolved Ticket Template

Use this when closing a ticket or closing the loop with the client after a fix has been completed.
Skip the acknowledgment — jump straight into what was done and what it means for them.

---

## Framework

[What was happening / what the issue was — one plain-English sentence]

[What Running Robots did to fix it — keep it simple, no technical jargon. Focus on what it means for the client, not the mechanics of the fix.]

Everything should be back to normal on your end. If you notice anything off or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out.

[Sign-off]

Larissa Simbro
Running Robots Support
support@therunningrobots.com

---

## Example

We noticed an issue with your site's contact form — submissions weren't going through, which meant messages from your customers weren't reaching you.

We've gone in and corrected the settings causing the problem. Your form is back up and running, and messages should be coming through without any issues now.

Everything should be back to normal on your end. If you notice anything off or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out.

Thanks,

Larissa Simbro
Running Robots Support
support@therunningrobots.com

---

## Notes
- Keep it to 2–3 short paragraphs max
- Translate any technical fix into what it means for the client's day-to-day
- Always end with an invitation to follow up
- Adjust sign-off tone to match the client (Thanks, Talk soon, etc.)
