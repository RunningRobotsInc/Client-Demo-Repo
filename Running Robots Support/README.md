# Running Robots Support

This folder demonstrates how Running Robots handles client support. It mirrors how our internal support specialist actually operates — the team structure, reply patterns, escalation paths, and tools we use to keep client communication clear and predictable.

## What's in here

- **SKILL.md** — Loadable skill describing how to engage Running Robots support, what to include in a support request, and what to expect in response. Paste this into your AI assistant alongside any ticket or follow-up question for support-aware guidance.
- **Templates/** — The three reply patterns we standardize on so communication is predictable: pending work, resolved tickets, and escalations.

## How to reach us

- **Email:** [`support@therunningrobots.com`](mailto:support@therunningrobots.com) — single front door; every email creates or updates a Zoho Desk ticket.
- **Self-service knowledge base:** [support.therunningrobots.com/portal/en/kb/website-articles](https://support.therunningrobots.com/portal/en/kb/website-articles) — searchable articles on common website questions.
- **Look in [`website/`](../website/) first** — your monthly site performance reports often answer "is this normal?" or "did something change?" directly. Point your AI assistant at the most recent `website/YYYY-MM-website.md` file before escalating.

## How support is delivered

- **Larissa Simbro** — Support Specialist. First point of contact on every ticket; owns client communication.
- **Alex** — Technical fix. Handles the actual work on the site, code, integrations, and configuration.
- **Tif** — Relationship-level contact. Handles escalations, scope and billing conversations, and anything beyond a standard reply.
- **Ticketing:** Zoho Desk

See [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) for the full workflow.
