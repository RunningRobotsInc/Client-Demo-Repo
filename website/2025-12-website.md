## Summary (AI-generated)

December was the slowest month of 2025, as expected for a B2B manufacturer in a holiday/year-end shutdown window. Sessions of **8,022** are down **9.1% MoM** but **3.2% higher YoY** vs. December 2024. RFQ submissions of **38** softened in line with sessions. Two notable wins this month: (1) the **2026 ISO renewal summary** blog post drove 412 sessions in two weeks and surfaced for "iso 9001 metal fabricator midwest" by Dec 22; (2) Direct traffic stayed strong (33% of total sessions), suggesting existing customers are using the site for repeat ordering despite the seasonal slowdown.
