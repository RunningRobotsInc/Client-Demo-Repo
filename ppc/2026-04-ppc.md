# Prairie Ridge Manufacturing — PPC Performance

_Reporting period: April 1–30, 2026_
_Prepared: May 4, 2026 by Running Robots_
_Reviewed and approved by: Meegan Campbell_

---

## Summary (AI-generated)

April PPC spend totaled **$8,420** across six active campaigns, generating **1,930 sessions**, **48 RFQ submissions**, and **8 closed-won deals** (close-rate measured at 90 days post-click). Cost-per-lead averaged **$175.42** — a **17% improvement** over March, driven mostly by the 5-axis CNC Launch campaign hitting its stride. The **Competitor — Tier-1 Fabricator** campaign continues to underperform: a CPL of **$542.10**, well above target, and a 5.2% bounce-on-landing rate that suggests intent mismatch. We recommend pausing it and reallocating budget to the 5-axis CNC and Laser Cutting campaigns. Branded campaign efficiency is excellent (CPL **$22.18**), and the Geo — Iowa Engineering Buyers campaign is now the second-best performer.

## Search-term insights (auto-mined queries we paid for)

Notable customer searches that triggered our ads in April:

- "metal fabrication for food packaging line" — 14 clicks, 2 RFQs (CVR 14.3%) — recommend adding a Food Processing landing page tighter to the wash-down/sanitary spec.
- "5 axis cnc for ag bracket" — 9 clicks, 1 RFQ — strong intent, worth its own ad group.
- "prairie ridge 5 axis vs 3 axis" — 22 clicks, 4 RFQs — capability-vs-capability searches outperforming.
- "prairie ridge laser cutting capacity" — 18 clicks, 0 RFQs — we're sending these to /capabilities. Should land on /capabilities/laser-cutting directly with capacity callouts.
- "iowa metal fab reviews" — 12 clicks, 0 RFQs — bouncing because we don't have a reviews/testimonials page. Consider creating one.

Source: `supporting/google-ads-search-terms-2026-04.csv`

## Ad copy testing

Ran a 2-variant headline test on the 5-Axis CNC Launch campaign all month:

| Variant | Impressions | CTR | CVR |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — "5-Axis CNC Cells, Iowa-Built, ISO 9001" | 92,118 | 2.4% | 1.9% |
| B — "Quotes Back In 3 Days. Parts Delivered On Time." | 92,102 | 2.0% | 1.4% |

Variant A wins. Pausing B in May, scaling A across other ad groups.

## Landing page CRO observations

- `/capabilities/cnc-machining` — 1.4% campaign CVR, 4.2% organic CVR. Paid traffic converts at half the rate. Hypothesis: paid visitors are colder. May test a lighter-touch ad-group landing page with a "Get Capability Sheet" lead magnet ahead of full RFQ.
- `/quote` — 18.6% CVR overall, but PPC-source CVR is 4.1%. PPC visitors don't go straight to RFQ — they explore capability pages first. This is normal for B2B engineering buyers; just confirms attribution math.

---

## What we recommend for May

1. **Pause Competitor — Tier-1 Fabricator** campaign. Reallocate $500/month to 5-Axis CNC Launch.
2. **Add 8 negative keywords** listed above (estimated savings: $400/month).
3. **Build "Food Processing Fabrication" landing page** — search-term mining shows clear demand from sanitary / wash-down spec buyers.
4. **Direct "prairie ridge laser cutting capacity" exact-match clicks to laser-cutting capability page**, not the capabilities overview.
5. **Increase 5-Axis CNC Launch daily budget** from $80 to $110/day starting May 5.
