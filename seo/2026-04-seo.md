# Prairie Ridge Manufacturing — SEO Performance

_Reporting period: April 1–30, 2026_
_Prepared: May 4, 2026 by Running Robots_
_Reviewed and approved by: Meegan Campbell_

---

## Summary (AI-generated)

Organic traffic grew **24.1% MoM** on the back of the **5-axis CNC machining capability launch**, with the new pages claiming first-page rankings for **17 commercial-intent queries** in just six weeks. Total clicks from Search Console: **6,894** (vs. 5,556 in March). Average position improved from 13.4 to **11.8**. The biggest movers were "5 axis cnc machining iowa" (now #2, was unranked) and "metal fabrication iowa" (now #4, was #7). Two concerns: (1) impressions for "custom metal brackets" dropped **18% MoM** as a new competitor in southern Iowa pushed updated comparison content, and (2) the AEO pull rate on Google's AI overviews held at **8% of queries** — flat for four consecutive months. Recommend doubling down on capability schema and FAQ-driven content in May to recapture share on the brackets cluster.

## On-page SEO audit

Running Robots ran a full Search Atlas on-page audit on April 28. Findings:

- **17 pages** with title tags exceeding 60 characters — flagged for tightening.
- **9 pages** missing meta descriptions (mostly older blog posts).
- **6 pages** with H1 mismatch vs. target keyword (older /resources content).
- **4 capability pages** missing FAQ schema — the new CNC machining pages are clean, older laser cutting and welding pages need it.
- **2 pages** with image alt text gaps.

All 38 issues are queued in Zoho Projects under the "SEO – Q2 cleanup" task list. Lead engineer: Alex (Running Robots).

Source: `supporting/search-atlas-audit-2026-04.csv`

## What we recommend for May

1. **Recapture share on "custom metal brackets"** — refresh `/capabilities` cluster with updated 2026 capacity stats, lead times, and tolerance bands. Add comparison schema.
2. **Add FAQ schema to laser cutting and welding capability pages** — the CNC machining pages with FAQ are pulling 2x the AI Overviews mentions.
3. **Tighten 17 long title tags** — quick win, queued in Zoho Projects.
4. **Pursue 5 backlink targets** — Iowa Economic Development Authority, two ag-equipment trade publications, and two engineering-supplier directories.
